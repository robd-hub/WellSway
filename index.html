Coming to your first class

Everyone starts somewhere. Here's what to expect, so you can walk in feeling relaxed.

Message me to book
  1. Message me first. A quick WhatsApp or call so I know to expect you. Let me know if you've danced before.
  2. Wear something comfy. Clothes you can move in and smooth-soled shoes. Trainers are fine for Salsa Fit.
  3. Arrive a few minutes early. Then we can say hello before we start. Bring a bottle of water.
  4. Enjoy it. I teach every step slowly, and nobody expects you to get it right first time. It's £10 on the night.

My dance journey

Dance has been a huge part of my life for as long as I can remember. I started dancing when I was just five years old, but my love for movement began even earlier. My parents always say that ever since I learned to walk, I was dancing!

I was born in Wrocław, Poland, and my parents found a local dance studio where they introduced me to the wonderful world of dance. From that moment my journey began, and dance quickly became one of the most important parts of my life.

Over the years, I have had the opportunity to take part in many competitions across Poland and Europe, gaining valuable experience both on and off the dance floor. One of my proudest achievements was competing at the French Open in Paris, where I placed 13th in the Junior category. I also finished 4th in Poland as a junior.

Now I'd love to share that joy with you, here in Washingborough.

Good to know

Do I need a partner?

No. Come on your own, with a partner or with a friend. Everyone gets to dance.

I've never danced before. Which class should I join?

Tuesday Beginners (19:00–20:00) is made for you. If you just want a fun workout, try Monday's Salsa Fit for Ladies.

What age can children start?

Kids' classes on Saturday at 9am are for children aged 5 and up, and all abilities are welcome.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in and smooth-soled shoes. For Salsa Fit, trainers and gym wear are perfect.

How much do classes cost?

Adult classes (Salsa Fit, Beginners and Improvers) are £10 per person per class. Kids' classes are £7 per child.

Do you offer private classes?

Yes, private classes are available. For times and prices, message me on WhatsApp or call 07753 612174.
